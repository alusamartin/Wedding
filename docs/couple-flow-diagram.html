Your wedding on Everafter Planner

Who does what — the couple, the planner, the vendors, and the parts the system handles on its own.

1 · The four players

👰‍♀️ Couple (you)

Owns the wedding · logs in to your own portal
  • Registers the wedding (date, names, contact)
  • Sets the guest list, sees the budget, ticks the checklist
  • Browses the marketplace and sends enquiries to vendors
  • Shares the wedding website & collects RSVPs
  • Sees every payment and receipt

🧑‍💼 Wedding planner

Your account manager · assigned by the office per wedding
  • Confirms your date, package and price band
  • Negotiates with vendors and confirms the deal
  • Owns the calendar, budget, suppliers and reminders
  • Sends the SMS invitations and chases RSVPs
  • Is the only one who can sign off money out

🍽️ Vendor

Caterer, decor, photo, MC, cake, venue, transport…
  • Lists a profile and priced packages (KSh)
  • Receives enquiries tied to your wedding
  • Accepts or declines, sees only its own deals
  • Delivers on the day, then is marked completed
  • Is rated by you afterwards

⚙️ The system (no human)

Does the chasing nobody wants to do
  • Creates the booking, checklist and calendar on sign-up
  • Notifies the right person at every status change
  • Texts 60 / 30 / 7 days before the wedding about balances
  • Verifies M-Pesa payments automatically
  • Keeps every role's view scoped to their own wedding

3 · The whole wedding, stage by stage

Stage You (couple) Your planner Vendor / system
1Register Fill the form on the home page: names, phone, email, password, preferred date. Nothing yet — the request sits in the office's Pending queue. System checks the date is free, creates your account, your booking and a draft checklist.
2Confirmed Get the "your date is confirmed" bell + SMS. Your portal tools unlock. Fills in bride/groom, package (KSh 50k–350k), venue, date, and assigns herself as your organizer. System emails/SMSes you, seeds the Kenyan milestones (ruracio, banns, eCitizen, committee).
3Plan Guest list, budget view, checklist ticks, installation plan, your wedding website. Owns the master calendar, budget, headcounts per function, venue sheet and supplier notes. System sends RSVP nudges and deadline reminders; headcount checks flag a package too small.
4Enquire Browse vendors by category + county, open a package, send a message. Gets a bell with your enquiry and the context — and starts negotiating. Vendor gets an SMS heads-up + the enquiry in their ledger as pending.
5Book money See the status live: pending → confirmed. No hidden side deals. Enters the agreed amount and confirms the deal; commission (10%, 15% premium) is recorded. Vendor accepts or declines from its own dashboard; the couple and planner are notified either way.
6Pay money Pay by cash, M-Pesa (code recorded) or bank; see budget vs actual and download receipts. Records/liquidates each payment, watches the balance and the deposit alerts. System verifies the M-Pesa code against what Safaricom reported; texts reminders at 60/30/7 days.
7The day Guests arrive via the website; RSVPs and the transport/accommodation list are already sorted. Runs the running order: ruracio, church, reception, send-off. Vendors deliver the function their deal covers; deals move to completed.
8After Rate each vendor, download the gallery, share your wedding page. Closes the budget, finalises photos, can publish the wedding as a showcase. Ratings feed the marketplace; completed deals feed the public real-weddings blog.

4 · Who pays whom

You → planner

The package price (KSh 50k–350k) plus any add-ons such as the wedding website at KSh 1,999. Tracked payment by payment, with receipts.

You → vendors

Whatever you agree for each service. It is recorded on the deal so the budget, the balance and the receipts all agree.

Vendor → platform

A 10% commission on each confirmed deal (15% for premium listings). You never pay this separately — it comes out of the vendor's side.

One login door for everybody · each account sees only its own wedding, its own deals, its own money.
